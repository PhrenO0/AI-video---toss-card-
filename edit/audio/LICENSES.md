# Audio sources and license notes

## Mixkit source recordings

- **Police short whistle** — Mixkit SFX 615, downloaded as WAV from `https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/615/615.wav`. Used as four short cuts: two apparent gate-rejection sounds and the matching two referee-whistle sounds.
- **Subway interior ambience** — Mixkit SFX 2680, downloaded as WAV from `https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/2680/2680.wav`. Used quietly beneath the full edit.

## Original edit-generated SFX

The pocket whoosh, card-declaration shimmer, footsteps, success tag chime, and end-brand resolve are synthesized locally with FFmpeg and do not use third-party recordings.
